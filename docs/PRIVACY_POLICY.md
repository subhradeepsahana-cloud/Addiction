# Privacy Policy (placeholder)

_This is a placeholder for App Store / Play Store submission. Replace with a lawyer-reviewed policy before public launch._

Sober Companion collects the information you provide (drink logs, cravings, check-ins, My Why photos and descriptions, preferences) to power the app's tracking and personalization features. Data is stored in Supabase (Postgres + Storage), protected by row-level security so only you can access your own data. AI features send a minimal, explicitly-assembled context summary (never your raw history or photos) to OpenAI to generate responses; OpenAI does not receive your account identity.

You can export your data (Settings → Export my data) or delete your account and all associated data at any time (Settings → Delete my account). Location and calendar access are optional, off by default, and can be disabled at any time. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
